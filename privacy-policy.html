Kenvori Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Kenvori operates digital products and related content across multiple niches. This Privacy Policy explains how Kenvori handles information in connection with its websites, digital products, and integrations, including the Pinterest API.

Information we may process

Kenvori may process information that is necessary to operate its own accounts and publish or manage content. Through the Pinterest API, this may include account identifiers, boards, Pins, publishing status, and performance or analytics data made available by Pinterest.

How we use information

Information is used only to operate, automate, measure, and improve Kenvori's own content, digital products, and marketing activities. This includes creating and scheduling Pins, managing published content, and reviewing performance data to improve future content.

Sharing and sale of data

Kenvori does not sell personal information or Pinterest account data. Information is not shared with third parties except where needed to provide technical services, comply with legal obligations, protect rights and security, or when required by a platform integration.

Data retention and security

Kenvori keeps information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Pinterest integration

Kenvori uses the Pinterest API to publish and manage content on Kenvori-controlled Pinterest accounts and, where permitted, retrieve related reporting or analytics data. Use of Pinterest data is subject to Pinterest's applicable terms and policies.

Your choices

Where applicable, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with Kenvori. You may also revoke permissions granted to connected platforms through the relevant platform settings.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be published at this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Kenvori through the contact method provided on its active storefront or official account.
